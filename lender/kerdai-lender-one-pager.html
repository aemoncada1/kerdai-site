The report your credit file needs

02 / How it works

Lender engages, borrower pays at closing

  1. You sign the engagement letter. The report is addressed to the bank and the bank is the client.
  2. Borrower pays the fee at closing as a loan cost. No retainer from the bank.
  3. Day 1: document request goes to the buyer and seller. We collect directly, you stay copied.
  4. Day 5: findings call with your BDO and underwriter. If earnings are coming in light, you hear it here, not on day 10.
  5. Day 10: signed report delivered, counted from a complete document set, with workpapers available on request.

Independence statement

No stake in the deal closing

Kerdai accepts lender-commissioned Quality of Earnings engagements only from the lender. We do not accept engagements from the borrower or the seller on the same transaction, and we are paid a fixed fee that does not change with the outcome of the loan.

Disclosure of common ownership. Kerdai's principals also own IC-USA, a business brokerage. Kerdai declines any lender-commissioned work on a transaction where IC-USA or its agents represent the buyer or the seller, and we confirm this in writing in every engagement letter before work begins.

If a conflict surfaces after engagement, we withdraw, return any fee and refer the bank to another provider.

What the bridge gives your underwriter

Earnings you can put in the DSC

Every line is traced. Each adjustment on the bridge points to a bank statement, invoice, tax return or payroll record in the workpapers, so a reviewer can follow it without calling us.

Unsupported revenue comes out. Months where reported sales do not tie to deposits are reduced to what the deposits support, with the gap explained.

Undocumented add-backs are not counted, however reasonable they sound. The schedule lists what was claimed, what was accepted and why, and the result is stated as adjusted EBITDA and as cash flow available for debt service.

Can the bank rely on the report?

Yes. The engagement letter names the bank as the client and the party entitled to rely on the report for its credit decision and SBA file. The borrower gets a copy only with the bank's permission, under a non-reliance notice.

What if the valuation comes back different?

The QofE establishes the earnings, the valuation applies a multiple to them. If the appraiser needs a reconciliation to our figure, or the structure changes and the bank needs a revised bridge, we issue a revision at no added fee within 30 days of the report date.

What happens when add-backs fail?

You hear it on the day-5 call, not in the final report. We quantify the shortfall against projected DSC so the BDO can renegotiate price or structure before the credit memo is written. We do not soften a finding to keep a deal alive.
