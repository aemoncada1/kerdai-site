How it works

Five steps from inquiry to closing.

The same sequence on every listing on this marketplace. It protects the seller's confidentiality and gets a qualified buyer to the data room without wasted weeks.

STEP 1

Inquire

Use the BizBuySell link or the contact form on this page and name the listing. You hear back from the sell-side team by email.

Same or next business day
STEP 2

NDA and qualification

Sign the NDA and send a short bio, a personal financial statement with proof of funds, and a lender pre-qualification or funding letter.

Before any confidential information is shared
STEP 3

CIM and Q&A

You receive the confidential information memorandum and the financial summary, and send written questions. Answers come from the sell-side team, not from the business.

Please do not contact the business directly
STEP 4

IOI and LOI

A written non-binding indication of interest opens the data room and access to management. A letter of intent follows once price and structure are agreed.

Data room and management access
STEP 5

Diligence, financing and closing

Your diligence, your lender's underwriting and the purchase agreement, through closing. On SBA 7(a) acquisitions of $3 million or more the SBA requires a lender-commissioned Quality of Earnings report.

The group's affiliate Kerdai can prepare that report only when the group is not the seller's representative.
